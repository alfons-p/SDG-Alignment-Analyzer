What the model may not do

It writes sentences only — every figure on the page is rendered from the data, not from the model. It cannot name a council, rank one above another, or state that a council did or did not do something. Failing any check drops the draft and the current text stays live.

One admin account uploads and runs every report. Everyone else reads published results; the officer role is defined but not yet enabled.

Two thresholds worth knowing

Goal 11 sits low at 0.459 and Goal 17 at 0.059, so both catch a wide net of place-based and partnership language — that is why Goal 11 dominates most council reports. Goals 6 and 14 sit above 0.94 and almost never fire. Read counts for those four goals with that in mind.

What the tool does not do

It reads only what the report describes. A Goal with no aligned activity means the report did not describe qualifying work, not that the council did none. It does not verify claims, weigh activities by size or spend, or read tables, charts and images — activities are extracted from prose only.

Settings are fixed for this analysis. Changing them re-runs the classifier and produces a new result set rather than editing this one.
